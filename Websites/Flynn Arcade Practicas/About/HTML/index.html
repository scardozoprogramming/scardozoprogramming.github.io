About

Description

We love creating games, and that’s what drives us every day.

Flynn’s Arcade is an independent game developer and publisher. We create our own games and bring exciting titles from talented indie developers to players around the world.

We believe great games can be simple, thoughtfully designed, and full of personality. Our goal is to deliver fun, engaging, and memorable gaming experiences.

Early history

Everything began several years ago at the Autonomous University of Barcelona, where Juan Peralta, CEO of Flynn’s Arcade, was teaching and supervising final-year degree projects related to video game development.

One of these projects was based on an ambitious idea: to develop a completely different kind of augmented reality (AR) mobile game that would reinvent the concept of traditional board games. This project was called OUTSIDERS.

OUTSIDERS combined elements of tabletop games like Warhammer with video games like XCOM, bringing them together through augmented reality. During the development of this project, Juan Peralta met Francisco Raya, one of its designers.

What came next

Currently, Juan Peralta holds a PhD in artificial intelligence, serves as a lecturer at the Polytechnic University of Valencia, and works as a researcher at the Institute of Automation and Industrial Informatics. He balances his teaching and research responsibilities with the development of new video game projects and the publishing of games from third-party studios.

That decision marked the beginning of Flynn’s Arcade and laid the foundation for the projects that followed. Since then, Francisco and Juan have continued working on new projects, with the goal of creating incredible and memorable gaming experiences for players.
