Full story

I wrote my first program as a kid in 1982, on my grandmother’s black and white TV, with a Sinclair Spectrum 48K, before moving on to the Commodore 64. It was an amazing experience and from that day on I knew I wanted to make games. I run pixel games, an independent development studio based in Luxembourg, focused on creating fun, classic, arcade experiences for modern machines.
