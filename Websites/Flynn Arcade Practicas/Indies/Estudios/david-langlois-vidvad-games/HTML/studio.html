David Langlois, Vidvad Games

Quebec-based creator of neo-retro arcade games and speedrunning.

Full story: David Langlois, Vidvad Games

Hello! I’m David and I’m a solo indie developer from Quebec City, Canada. I like to create neo-retro arcade games just like what I used to play growing up in the 90’s. I keep things short and simple and as a speedrunner myself, I really enjoy adding a twist where you’re rewarded for completing my games as fast as possible. With Vidvad Games, I do the programming, pixel art and game design, and I partner up with a good friend of mine (ZerolagGaming) for the music. I wish I could tell my younger self that one day, one of my creation would run on a Nintendo console. What a surreal experience!
