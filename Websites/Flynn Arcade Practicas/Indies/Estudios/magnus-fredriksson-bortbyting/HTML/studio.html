Full story: Magnus Fredriksson, Bortbyting

I’m Magnus Fredriksson, animator, filmmaker, and now indie game developer, thanks to a mild midlife crisis. I grew up in Kiruna in northern Sweden (where I now live again), where arcades were few and far between. Gaming at home on the Amiga or NES became my go-to, but I’ve always had a special love for arcade-style games that are easy to pick up and play, especially if they’re co-op.
