Luke Orsie, Crashable Studios

Two brothers drawing inspiration from their 1990s gaming.

Full story

I’m Luke Orsie, animator/artist for crashable studios. Besides making games I enjoy skateboarding, fishing, firearms, Dogs, cats, bush-crafting, and most non-traditional sports. Crashable Studios is comprised of us two brothers, Luke (me) and Micah. We have been gamers since we were kids in the 90’s. I remember firing up «Contra» on our brand new NES we got for Christmas in that time. Those memories and gaming moments have stuck with us through the years and we strive to recreated those magic moments/feelings we get from gaming in our own creative way. We have been developing since 2012 when our first game «Alum» was kickstarted and are on our third commercial title as of the writing of this bio.
