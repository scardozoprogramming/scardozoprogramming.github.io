About What Comes After

Party Game
Multiplayer
Action

A fast-paced multiplayer party game where players compete and cooperate through a variety of exciting mini-games set inside themed rooms. Each challenge tests different skills, including teamwork, communication, concentration, problem-solving, and emotional resilience.
Do you have what it takes to outplay your opponents, master every room, and become the ultimate RoomBattle champion?

Inspired by the fun and unpredictability of modern party games, RoomBattle combines accessible gameplay with meaningful experiences that promote positive social interaction and mental well-being. Every match offers new challenges, encouraging players to adapt, collaborate, and overcome obstacles together.

Features

  • A wide variety of fast-paced mini-games with unique gameplay mechanics.
  • Competitive and cooperative game modes for friends and family.
  • Dynamic scoring system and round-based progression.
  • Themed rooms, each introducing new challenges and objectives.
  • Gameplay elements inspired by communication, teamwork, and emotional growth.
  • Colorful low-poly visual style designed for accessibility and fun.
  • Highly replayable matches with randomized mini-game selection.
  • Progressive difficulty to keep the experience engaging.
  • Local and online multiplayer support.
  • Designed to encourage collaboration, empathy, and healthy social interaction.
