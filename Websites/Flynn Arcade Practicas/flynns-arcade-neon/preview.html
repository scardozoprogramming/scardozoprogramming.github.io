About Flynn's Arcade

Description

Flynn's Arcade develops original games and publishes titles by other creators. The company focuses on accessible, carefully designed games that give players an enjoyable experience.

Early History

The project began at the Autonomous University of Barcelona, where Juan Peralta taught and supervised video-game degree projects. With Felipe Lumbreras and student Xavier Alsina, the team explored an augmented-reality mobile game inspired by board games.

Developers and artists came together to create OUTSIDERS, a prototype combining tabletop strategy and video games with augmented reality. During that project, Juan met designer Francisco Raya.

After That

After completing the OUTSIDERS demo, team members moved on to different projects. Juan and Francisco continued developing new ideas, leading to Flynn's Arcade and further game projects.
